## Automation Opportunity Analysis

### Why These Salons Need Automation:

1. **Phone-Only Booking** - Staff tied up answering calls, missed appointments
2. **Walk-In Only** - No way to manage capacity or reduce wait times
3. **Basic/No Website** - Missing online customers who book digitally
4. **Mobile Services** - Critical need for scheduling and route planning
5. **Multi-Location** - Need centralized booking across sites

### Recommended Automation Solutions:

1. **Online Booking** - Fresha, Treatwell, Booksy, Salon IQ
2. **Client Management** - Track preferences, visit history, spend
3. **Appointment Reminders** - SMS/email to reduce no-shows (avg 20% reduction)
4. **Staff Scheduling** - Manage rotas, holidays, commissions
5. **Marketing Automation** - Birthday offers, rebooking prompts
6. **Payment Processing** - Deposits, prepayments, contactless

### Business Case Points:

- Average salon loses £5,000-£10,000/year to no-shows
- Online booking increases new client acquisition by 30%
- Automated reminders reduce no-shows by 40%
- Digital payments speed up checkout by 50%
