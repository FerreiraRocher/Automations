## SUMMARY & HONEST ASSESSMENT

**What the day actually offers:** 22 August 2026 is a large fixture date by volume — roughly 30+ matches across the target leagues — but an unusually poor one by *information quality*. Four of the requested leagues are playing their first match of the season, one has not started, and the Championship is one game in.

**Where the genuine edges are:**
1. **Inter v Monza** is the standout. A champion side that won by 11 points with the league's best attack, at home, against the weakest of three promoted teams, with managerial continuity. This is the one selection that would qualify as HIGH confidence under the framework even with full mid-season data.
2. **Brentford v Tottenham** carries the best team-news edge — Spurs are without both their club captain (Van de Ven) and vice-captain (Porro), a first-choice centre-back and right-back, away at a side that finished 12 points above them.
3. **Hull v Man Utd** has the largest raw resource gap but the most injury noise (Ugarte out for the season, De Ligt short of fitness, Šeško and Mount doubtful), which is why it sits on double chance.

**Where I would not stake:** anything in the Championship, League One, League Two, or the Turkish Süper Lig on this date, and anything in the leagues whose fixture lists could not be confirmed.

**The single biggest risk across every acca here:** opening-day variance. Newly promoted sides at home on matchday 1 in front of a full stadium routinely outperform their season-long baseline, and pre-season fitness in a World Cup summer is unusually uneven. The double-chance weighting throughout this report is a deliberate response to that, not conservatism for its own sake.
