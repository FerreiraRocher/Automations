## Platform Comparison for Getting Started

| Platform | Best For | Fees | Tips |
|----------|----------|------|------|
| **Fiverr** | Quick fixed-price gigs, chatbots, dashboards | 20% flat | Start at $30-$85, scale up. 24-48hr delivery wins. |
| **Upwork** | Ongoing contracts, automation work, higher-value projects | 20% first $500, then 10%, then 5% | Send 20 tailored proposals/day. Video intro = 30% more views. |
| **Toptal** | Premium $60-$200+/hr gigs | 0% (but top 3% acceptance) | Apply once you have a portfolio. Rigorous 4-week vetting. |
| **Contra** | Less competitive for new profiles | Lower fees | Good for beginners |
| **Direct/Gumroad** | Selling skill packages and templates | Minimal | Build audience via content |
