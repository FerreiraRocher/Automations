## Family Profile & Considerations

### Children's Ages & Needs

| Child | Age | Key Considerations |
|-------|-----|-------------------|
| **Child 1** | 6 years | School-age, can handle 2-3 hour activities, loves interactive experiences, needs mental stimulation |
| **Child 2** | 2 years | Toddler, needs nap times (usually 1-3pm), short attention span, loves sensory play, soft play areas |
| **Child 3** | 8 months | Baby, needs regular feeds, nappy changes, quiet spaces for naps, pushchair-accessible venues |

### Planning Principles

- **Nap-friendly scheduling**: Plan activities around baby/toddler nap times (avoid 1-3pm intensive activities)
- **Pushchair accessibility**: All venues must accommodate pushchairs
- **Indoor backup options**: Always have wet weather alternatives
- **Travel time**: Maximum 1.5 hours for day trips (younger children tolerate poorly)
- **Feeding facilities**: Baby changing, bottle warming, high chairs required
- **Early starts**: Arrive at attractions at opening to avoid crowds and maximise nap flexibility

### SUMMER HOLIDAYS - 2 WEEK TRIP

**Why This Works for Your Family:**

- Evening kids clubs so parents get dinner time

**Eurocamp Benefits:**

- **Toddler pools**: Dedicated splash areas
- **Kids clubs**: Ages 5+ (6yr-old sorted!)
- **Evening entertainment**: Family-friendly shows

**Practical Information:**
| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Travel option A** | Drive (Eurotunnel) - 10-12 hours with stops |

| **Cost estimate** | £2,000-3,500 for 2 weeks including travel |

### Money-Saving Tips

1. **Annual passes**: Sundown Adventureland Fun Pass (£60) pays for itself in 3 visits
2. **National Trust membership**: ~£130/year for family, free parking at NT properties
3. **Railcard**: Family & Friends Railcard (£30/year) = 1/3 off train fares
4. **Book early**: Eurocamp/hotels 20%+ cheaper booked 6+ months ahead
5. **Off-peak**: Avoid bank holidays for UK attractions
6. **Pack lunches**: Save £20-30 per trip vs. venue cafés
7. **Free attractions**: Wollaton Park, Attenborough, city farms - budget-friendly weekends
8. **Cashback sites**: TopCashback/Quidco for holiday bookings
