## How to Reach Clients Directly

### Strategy 1: Hacker News "Who is Hiring"
- Companies post with their **email addresses** directly
- Best for: Full-time remote or contract positions
- Tip: Search the thread for keywords like "python", "automation", "remote"

### Strategy 2: Freelancer.com Project Bidding
- Bid on projects to unlock **client chat**
- After completing a project, ask for direct contact for future work
- Many clients prefer off-platform work to avoid fees

### Strategy 3: LinkedIn Outreach
- Find the **hiring manager** or **CTO** at companies posting jobs
- Send connection request with personalized message
- Reference their job posting and your relevant experience

### Strategy 4: Study Fiverr Reviews
- Look at reviews on top sellers' profiles
- Identify **company names** mentioned in reviews
- Research those companies and reach out directly

### Strategy 5: PeoplePerHour
- Clients often post with **more visible details**
- Some projects include company websites
- Use company websites to find contact emails
