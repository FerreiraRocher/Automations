## SCOUT NOTES

### Top 5 Takeaways

1. **Begraoui is the biggest under-the-radar find** — 17 league goals for 7th-placed Estoril. No one outside Portugal is talking about him. At 24, he's in his prime development window and could be available for well under €10M.

2. **Porto's attack is generational** — Samu (13G) and Mora (18 years old) form one of Europe's most exciting young attacking duos. Porto lead the league on 65 points.

3. **Benfica's squad depth under 24 is unmatched** — Araújo, Silva, Sudakov, Schjelderup, Ivanović, Cabral, and Banjaqui. Seven players under 24 who could start at any club in Portugal.

4. **Gustavo Sá (Famalicão) rejected Saudi money** — A 21-year-old Portuguese U21 international who turned down €20M from Al-Ittihad. That signals serious ambition and confidence. He's targeting a top-5 European league move.

5. **The 2007 generation is historic** — Mora, Quenda, Simões, Felicíssimo, and Cabral (2008) are all contributing at senior level aged 18-19. This is Portugal's strongest youth cohort since the Ronaldo/Quaresma era.
