**Market Signal:** Active Upwork jobs for Voice AI booking platforms for restaurants, hospitality
**Product Concept:** White-label AI voice assistant that handles inbound calls, schedules appointments, answers FAQs
- **Target Market:** Restaurants, hotels, spas, medical clinics, service businesses
- **Key Features:**

  - Visual workflow builder
- **Pricing Model:** Freemium, $49-199/month
